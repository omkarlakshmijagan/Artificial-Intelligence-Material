\section*{Project Guidelines}
\begin{itemize}\item \textbf{Mini projects} (individual, 2 weeks): one algorithm, one dataset/simulation, a short report.
\item \textbf{Major projects} (teams of 3--4, 6 weeks): working system, demo video, GitHub repository, IEEE-format report.
\item Every project must include a \emph{README}, a requirements file, reproducible notebooks and a responsible-AI note.\end{itemize}
